Quantum-Selective Solid-State Energy Conversion Cell (H2DE)

DOI: 10.26434/chemrxiv.15006052/v2
  • Designed a catalyst-free \(H_2\) dissociation cell utilizing tapered Magnesium nanotips to concentrate built-in Schottky interface electric fields.
  • Formulated 1D WKB quantum tunneling models through monolayer hexagonal Boron Nitride (h-BN), achieving 100% proton selectivity over molecular hydrogen.
  • Validated structural deflection and multi-phase fluid flow across the cell architecture using CalculiX FEA and OpenFOAM CFD.

Thermoneutral Ionic Liquid Hydrogen Storage (LOHC)

DOI: 10.26434/chemrxiv.15007929/v1
  • Screened Guanidinium Bis(borane)amide derivatives achieving a high gravimetric hydrogen storage capacity of 11.7 wt%.

Design, Co-Simulation & Optimization of a 2.45 GHz Rectenna on Rogers RO4003

DOI: Forthcoming Submission · Technical Report Completed
  • Designed a high-efficiency microstrip patch antenna centered at 2.45 GHz on Rogers RO4003 substrate (\(\epsilon_r = 3.55\)) with an intermediate 1.5 mm air spacer to maximize radiation bandwidth.
  • Conducted 3D electromagnetic simulations in Ansys HFSS, verifying a resonant return loss \(S_{11} = -19.3\text{ dB}\) at 2.44 GHz (\(Z_{11} = 52.73 - j10.86\ \Omega\)).
  • Executed non-linear transient co-simulation in Ngspice using an HSMS-2860 Schottky diode and an optimized \(10\text{ nH}\) series inductor matching network.
  • Achieved peak RF-to-DC conversion efficiency of 59.62% and \(1.456\text{ V}\) DC output across a \(200\ \Omega\) load at \(+12.5\text{ dBm}\); completed KiCad PCB layout and manufacturing Gerbers.

Real-Time Forensic Graph Analytics for Anti-Money Laundering Detection

Codebase: github.com/Atlas55kk/Cyber_fraud_detector
  • Mathematically formalized dynamic multi-token multigraphs with taint conservation and temporal velocity decay laws for account-based blockchains (EVM & TRON).
  • Formulated an adaptive Priority Heuristic Search algorithm bounding state traversal to polynomial time while retaining 100% of primary laundering conduits.
  • Engineered a decoupled multi-threaded backend streaming progressive graph states via Server-Sent Events (SSE) to an interactive Cytoscape whiteboard (60 FPS rendering).
  • Validated against the July 2024 WazirX $230M exploit, achieving sub-second discovery latency, a 96.8% dust rejection rate, and an optimal detection \(F_1\)-score of 0.985.
