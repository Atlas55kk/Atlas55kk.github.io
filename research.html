A Closed-Loop Hydrogen Carrier Ecosystem: From Ocean Desalination to Vehicle Propulsion via a Recyclable Boron–Nitrogen–Carbon Liquid Fuel

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23284196
  • Formulated the complete end-to-end system architecture of the Ocean-to-Drive-Shaft (OTD) Pipeline: converting seawater to pumpable liquid fuel via solar-thermal desalination and green electrolysis.
  • Synthesized a recyclable B–N–C ionic liquid carrier (\(\text{MMG-GBBA}\), \(10.27\text{ wt}\%\ H_2\)) with zero vapor pressure and non-toxicity, eliminating the thermal penalties and boil-off risks of conventional LOHCs.
  • Engineered an on-board catalytic dehydrogenation reactor at \(80^\circ\text{C}\) powered 100% by fuel cell waste heat (\(9.0\text{ kW}\) thermal duty, consuming just \(3\%\) of \(300\text{ kW}\) rejected heat).
  • Coupled inland fuel regeneration with coastal co-product sales (desalination brine minerals and \(\sim 8\text{ kg } O_2/\text{kg } H_2\)), yielding a delivered fuel cost of \(\text{USD } 1.80/\text{kg } H_2\) and \(\sim 92\%\) round-trip efficiency.

High-Efficiency 2.45 GHz Microstrip Rectenna for Ambient RF Energy Harvesting: Electromagnetic-Circuit Co-Simulation, Optimization & Implementation on Rogers RO4003C

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23299260
  • Designed an inset-fed 2.45 GHz microstrip patch antenna on low-loss Rogers RO4003C substrate (\(\epsilon_r = 3.55\)) with an intermediate air-cavity spacer, achieving \(S_{11} = -19.3\text{ dB}\) and complex impedance \(Z_{11} = 52.73 - j10.86\ \Omega\) in full-wave EM modeling.
  • Realized an optimal conjugate impedance match in transient SPICE co-simulation using a single \(10\text{ nH}\) series inductor alongside a \(100\text{ pF}\) series DC block to eliminate ground leakage.
  • Achieved a peak RF-to-DC conversion efficiency of 59.62% (\(1.456\text{ V}\) DC) across a \(200\ \Omega\) load at \(+12.5\text{ dBm}\) using an Avago HSMS-2860 Schottky diode.
  • Executed mathematical harmonic analyses, component tolerance sweeps, thermal sensitivity evaluations (\(-20^\circ\text{C}\) to \(+70^\circ\text{C}\)), IoT duty-cycle power budgets, and complete KiCad PCB manufacturing files.
