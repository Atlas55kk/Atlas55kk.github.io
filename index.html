a little about me

I am an undergraduate student in Electronics & Communication Engineering (VLSI Design) with a deep passion for solving real-world physical and engineering challenges.

My approach begins with first principles: breaking down complex systems—whether it’s a hydrogen-fueled engine architecture or quantum tunneling effect through nanomembranes—to understand how and why they work.

To move from theory to practical models, I design the system framework and actively integrate modern computational and AI-driven workflows to automate simulations, always verifying the physics, auditing failure points, and refining the engineering along the way.

I don't claim to know everything; I value learning, continuous iteration, and rigorous double-checking. My objective is to contribute this problem-solving discipline and eagerness to learn to engineering teams pushing the boundaries of scientific instrumentation.

Advanced Propulsion & Machine Design

Provisional Patent Registered Aerospace Propulsion Research

Hydro Dynamic Engine (HDE): Rim-Driven Hubless Electric Jet

Target: Distributed Electric Propulsion for High-Altitude UAVs

  • Developed an outer-rim-driven hubless electric ducted fan eliminating traditional 25–35% hub intake blockage with a 100% open central bypass conduit.
  • Engineered a hybrid active-passive magnetic bearing (APMB) suspension with a 30-pole-pair Halbach rotor array driven by a slotless stator.
  • Validated 18,000 RPM overspeed structural integrity: Toray T1100G carbon-fiber sleeve limits peak hoop stress to 2,664 MPa, guaranteeing an aerospace \(\text{FOS} = 1.31\).
  • Coupled steady-state Conjugate Heat Transfer (CHT) proving continuous stator winding hot-spots remain at 98.8°C (radial), well below the 150°C epoxy threshold.
Hubless Rim-Driven Halbach Array 18,000 RPM Conjugate Heat Transfer AMB Controls
Research Manuscript SAE Int. Journal of Engines

Direct-Injection Hydrogen Rotary Powertrain (H2-ICE)

660 cc Zero-Emission Wankel Powertrain with Closed-Loop Condensation

  • Exploited spatial thermal segregation of the Wankel architecture to naturally eliminate intake backfiring and pre-ignition inherent to hydrogen combustion.
  • Sculpted aerodynamic toroidal flank pockets forming a double-toroidal swirl vortex in OpenFOAM CFD, completing homogeneous fuel mixing within \(2.0\text{ ms}\).
  • Conducted 3D structural FEA in CalculiX under peak combustion pressure (\(56.97\text{ bar}\)), confirming Yield \(\text{FOS} = 6.81\) and deflections under \(15.28\ \mu\text{m}\).
  • Integrated a Ranque-Hilsch Vortex Tube with Neodymium ring magnets for paramagnetic \(O_2\) enrichment and diamagnetic \(N_2\) venting, targeting zero-NOx combustion.
OpenFOAM CFD CalculiX FEA 56.97 bar Peak Load Ranque-Hilsch Vortex Zero-NOx Cycle
