A Little about Me

I am an undergraduate student in Electronics & Communication Engineering (VLSI Design) with a deep passion for solving real-world physical and engineering challenges.

My approach begins with first principles: breaking down complex systems—whether it’s a hydrogen-fueled engine architecture or quantum tunneling effect through nanomembranes—to understand how and why they work.

To move from theory to practical models, I design the system framework and actively integrate modern computational and AI-driven workflows to automate simulations, always verifying the physics, auditing failure points, and refining the engineering along the way.

I don't claim to know everything; I value learning, continuous iteration, and rigorous double-checking. My objective is to contribute this problem-solving discipline and eagerness to learn to engineering teams pushing the boundaries of scientific instrumentation.

Where I Am & Where I Am Going

Research Trajectory & Future Experiments

2025 – Early 2026 · Phase I Completed Foundation

First-Principles Modeling & Six Published Preprints

Completed six independent computational research studies and open-source scientific engines across solid-state physics, chemical hydrogen storage, aerospace propulsion, RF electromagnetics, and graph analytics—each documented as a standalone work:

H2DE Quantum Solid-State Cell

Formulated 1D WKB proton tunneling across monolayer h-BN with tapered Mg nanotip Schottky field concentration (Published on ChemRxiv).

Thermoneutral LOHC Hydrogen Storage

Screened Guanidinium Bis(borane)amide derivatives (11.7 wt% \(H_2\)) via composite DFT (PBEh-3c) and GFN2-xTB in ORCA (Published on ChemRxiv).

Directed-Energy Atmospheric Thruster

Modeled pulsed isochoric propulsion using photonic crystal cavity evanescent wave extraction and Sedov–Taylor blast waves in OpenFOAM (Published on Zenodo).

Ocean-to-Drive-Shaft (OTD) Fuel Ecosystem

Designed a closed-loop hydrogen pipeline from solar seawater desalination to on-board \(80^\circ\text{C}\) waste-heat dehydrogenation at \(\$1.80/\text{kg } H_2\) (Published on Zenodo).

2.45 GHz Microstrip Rectenna (Rogers RO4003C)

Co-simulated an inset-fed patch antenna (\(S_{11} = -19.3\text{ dB}\)) and HSMS-2860 Schottky rectifier achieving 59.62% RF-to-DC efficiency with full KiCad Gerbers (Published on Zenodo).

Real-Time AML Forensic Graph Detector

Built a multi-token blockchain taint-tracking engine with priority heuristic traversal achieving an \(F_1\)-score of 0.985 on the WazirX exploit (Published on Zenodo).

GEO 3D Parametric Wireframe Engine

Developed an open-source 3D mathematical geometry and surface mesh visualization engine for rapid parametric verification prior to CFD/FEA meshing.
